Concrete Waves: A Skate Park Road Trip

By Lucas Ferreira — Skater, Writer

Six cities, six parks

The rule for this trip was one park a day, no exceptions, even on the day it rained in three of the six cities. We showed up anyway, mostly to stand under the one covered section and complain about the weather with strangers.

The best park nobody talks about

Not the biggest, not the most famous — just a mid-size bowl behind a rec center that happened to be empty at 4pm on a Tuesday. Sometimes that's the whole story.

Every good skate trip has one park you can't find on the map and one you wish you couldn't.

The notebook

Six cities in, the notebook is mostly bad sketches of ramp angles and one very confident wrong guess at a park's opening hours.
