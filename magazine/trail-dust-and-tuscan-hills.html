The map that lied

Every gravel road on the map was, apparently, a suggestion. We rerouted twice before lunch on day one, both times for the better — the wrong turn found us a vineyard neither guidebook mentioned.

Long lunches, longer afternoons

Three hours felt like the minimum acceptable lunch length by day three. Nobody argued.

The best route is rarely the one on the map. It's the one the map's wrong about.

What we brought home

A suitcase heavier than it left, and a rule for next time: always leave room for one more bottle.
