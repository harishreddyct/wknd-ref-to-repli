Cold Water, Clear Skies: Arctic Surf Notes

Priya Nandan
Photographer

Six millimeters of commitment

The wetsuit alone took longer to get into than most of our sessions lasted. Six millimeters of neoprene, a hood, boots, gloves — by the time you're in the water you've already decided you're staying in, whatever it takes.

The cold isn't the hard part. The hard part is remembering why you came.

The light makes up for it

What the water takes back, the sky gives back tenfold. Low sun for most of the day, and a kind of clarity to the air that made even the paddle-outs feel worth the ache in your hands.

Would we go back

Immediately, and with a thicker hood.
