Camper Vans and Coastlines

By Mara Dunlevy — Photographer, Writer

A slow start

We picked up the van on a Tuesday, which felt like an oddly formal way to start something this loose. No real itinerary — just a coastline, three weeks, and a cooler that would spend most of the trip half full of the wrong things.

The first two days were mostly spent figuring out where everything lived. The stove that folded out from under the bed. The mirror that only worked if you stood at a very specific angle. By day three we'd stopped noticing.

Wanderlust: a strong desire to travel, usually accompanied by a poorly packed van.

Tide pools and flat tires

Somewhere around the halfway point we lost most of a day to a flat tire outside a town whose name we still can't spell correctly. The upside: a tide pool twenty feet from the shoulder, and more hours than planned to sit and look at it.

What we'd do differently

Fewer clothes, more snacks, and a second set of van keys — we only lost the first set once, but once was enough. Otherwise, we'd do almost all of it exactly the same way, tire included.
