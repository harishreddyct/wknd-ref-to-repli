Chasing Winter Light: A Ski Touring Journal

By Theo Banks — Filmmaker, Writer

The wrong socks

First lesson of the trip, learned the hard way on hour one of a three-hour skin: cotton socks are not touring socks. The second lesson, learned shortly after: there is no graceful way to change socks on an exposed ridge in the wind.

Four good turns can justify an entire morning of walking uphill. Ask anyone who tours.

Four turns, one view

We only got four real turns in before the light went flat, but the view from the top — a whole valley still blue with early morning shadow — made the socks, the wind and the walk feel like a fair trade.

Next time

Wool socks, obviously. And maybe one fewer alarm before sunrise.
