What is WKND Adventures?

WKND Adventures is a small, contributor-run travel and adventure publication. We cover climbing, cycling, skiing, surfing and general travel through trip reports, gear notes and the occasional trailhead confession.

Who is it for?

Anyone who'd rather be outside. You don't need to climb 5.13 or surf double-overhead to belong here — most of our contributors are enthusiastic amateurs, not professionals.

How is WKND Adventures funded?

A mix of small sponsor partnerships with gear brands we actually use, and a members program that unlocks a handful of longer-form stories.

Can I contribute a story?

Yes — we're always looking for trip reports and guides from guest contributors. Reach out through the contact details in the footer with a pitch and a couple of photos.

How often do you publish?

Roughly one new article a week, plus a new guided trip listing every few weeks depending on the season.

When did WKND Adventures start?

We put up our first trip report in 2018, after two friends came back from a very disorganized climbing trip and decided the world needed a better packing list.

Do you ever get the gear wrong?

Constantly. See the Chasing Winter Light article for a detailed account of the wrong socks.

Is any of this real?

No — WKND Adventures is a fictitious publication. Every trip, contributor and article on this site is a placeholder built to demonstrate a component-based website structure, not an actual travel company.
