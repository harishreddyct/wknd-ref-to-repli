Overview

Five days moving slowly between hillside vineyards and small village markets, with more long lunches than scheduled activities. Minimal walking, maximum eating — this is the trip for the group that wants scenery without the effort.

Want to burn off some of those lunches? Two-Wheel Tour of the Southern Coast covers similar back roads, on a bike.

Itinerary

Days one and two

Settle into the first village, with an afternoon vineyard visit and tasting.

Days three through five

A slow loop through two more villages, market mornings, and a final long lunch on the last day.

What to bring
  • Comfortable walking shoes (light walking only, nothing technical)
  • An empty bag for the trip home — you will buy wine
  • Layers for cool evenings
  • An appetite
