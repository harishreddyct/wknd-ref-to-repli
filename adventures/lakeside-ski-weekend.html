Overview

A relaxed resort-based weekend built around a lake with a view most people only see in the summer. Good for mixed-ability groups — lift-served terrain during the day, and a shared cabin with a lake view for the evenings.

Looking for something more remote? Alpine Ski Traverse trades lifts for a hut-to-hut route.

Itinerary

Day one

Check in, gear check, and an afternoon on the groomed runs to get everyone's legs under them.

Day two

A full day on the mountain, finishing with a lakeside soak before the drive home.

What to bring
  • Skis or snowboard (rentals available at the resort)
  • Waterproof jacket and pants
  • Swimwear for the lake soak
  • Lift ticket budget (not included in trip price)
