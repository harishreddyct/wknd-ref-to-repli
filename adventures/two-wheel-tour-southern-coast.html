Overview

Four days riding the coast road south, with a support van carrying everything but your water bottles. Expect headwind on at least one afternoon and a fish taco stand worth the detour on day three.

Want dirt instead of pavement? Singletrack Mountain Bike Getaway is the trail version of this trip.

Itinerary

Days one and two

Coastal riding with a lunch stop at the halfway point each day, overnighting in small towns along the route.

Days three and four

The longest riding day, followed by a shorter, celebratory final leg into the finish town.

What to bring
  • Road bike, tuned before the trip (rentals available on request)
  • Helmet and cycling shoes/pedals compatible with your bike
  • Padded shorts — four days adds up
  • Rain jacket, just in case
