Overview

A guided two-day climb up one of the region's classic ridge lines, with a basecamp night between the two legs. Suited to climbers comfortable with multi-pitch movement — this isn't a first-time-outdoors trip, but it's not an expedition either.

If this one's booked out, Backcountry Basecamp Trek covers similar ground at a slightly easier grade.

Itinerary

Day one

Meet at the trailhead mid-morning, hike in to basecamp by early afternoon, and climb the lower pitches before dinner.

Day two

Early start for the summit pitches, lunch on the ridge, then a long descent back to the cars by late afternoon.

What to bring
  • Climbing harness and helmet (rental available on request)
  • Sturdy approach shoes, broken in before the trip
  • Warm layer for the basecamp night — it gets cold fast after sunset
  • Headlamp with spare batteries
  • Refillable water bottle, at least 2L capacity
