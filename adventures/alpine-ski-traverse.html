Overview

A three-day hut-to-hut traverse across an alpine ridge system, skinning up each morning and skiing back down through untracked bowls in the afternoon. Best suited to skiers with touring experience and a solid aerobic base.

Prefer something closer to the car? Lakeside Ski Weekend covers similar terrain over a single weekend.

Itinerary

Day one

Skin in from the trailhead to the first hut, with two laps on the way in.

Days two and three

The main traverse day, followed by a shorter exit day back to the cars via one last bowl.

What to bring
  • Touring skis or splitboard with skins, fitted before the trip
  • Avalanche safety kit (beacon, probe, shovel) — required, not optional
  • Hut layers: warm mid-layer, down jacket, dry socks for the evening
  • Sunscreen and glacier glasses
