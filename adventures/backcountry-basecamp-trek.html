Overview

Three days deep enough into the backcountry that your phone stays in your pack the whole time. A gentler introduction to multi-day trekking, with one optional scramble to a ridge viewpoint on the middle day.

Want more climbing and less carrying? Ridge Line Climbing Weekend is the sharper-edged version of this trip.

Itinerary

Day one

Hike in to basecamp, set up camp, and take an easy loop around the lake before dinner.

Days two and three

An optional ridge scramble on day two, then a relaxed hike out on day three.

What to bring
  • Backpacking pack (45–60L) and a tent rated for the season
  • Sleeping bag and pad suited to overnight lows
  • Water filter or purification tablets
  • Sturdy boots, broken in before the trip
