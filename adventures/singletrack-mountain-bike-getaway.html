Overview

Two days of flowy, well-built singletrack with just enough climbing to earn the descents. A guide rides with the group both days and knows exactly which fire road shortcut skips the worst of the grind back up.

Prefer road over trail? Two-Wheel Tour of the Southern Coast trades dirt for pavement.

Itinerary

Day one

A shorter loop to warm up, then lunch at the trailhead before an afternoon session on the main descent.

Day two

The full loop, including the ridge climb and its reward: the longest descent of the trip.

What to bring
  • Full-suspension or hardtail mountain bike in good working order
  • Helmet and gloves (required)
  • Hydration pack and a spare tube
  • Layers — mornings start cool even in summer
