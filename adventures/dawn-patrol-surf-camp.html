Overview

Four days of early wake-ups chasing the best light and the calmest water. Coaching is available every session but optional — plenty of the group just wants to surf.

Want colder water and fewer people? Alpine Ski Traverse swaps the wetsuit for skins, on a totally different kind of morning.

Itinerary

Days one and two

Dawn sessions each morning, with an easy beach day in the afternoon to recover.

Days three and four

A second, farther-flung beach on day three, then one last dawn session before heading home.

What to bring
  • Own board if you have one (rentals available)
  • Wetsuit or rash guard depending on water temperature
  • Reef-safe sunscreen
  • A second towel — you'll need it
